HERE product notice

When meaning gets lost

LinguaLens 35 can translate words in real time, but not every meaning is easy to translate. Cultural Context+ may interpret tone, humour or indirect language incorrectly.

Users can review the original speech when available and report a translation error.

Privacy note: Offline mode processes conversations locally, but some advanced contextual features may require a cloud connection.

When glasses are not available

Human interpreter

Clinics and selected public service centres can book a human interpreter for appointments where automatic translation is not suitable.

Shared devices

Public units can be borrowed at libraries and service points, but availability can be limited during busy periods.

Phone and screen mode

Basic captions and text translation can be used without wearing the glasses when a supported screen is available.

Availability check

Users can check nearby device availability before travelling to an appointment.
