AirHalo travel guide

Protection that travels with you

AirHalo uses a foldable filtration collar to create a small zone of filtered air around the wearer’s breathing area. The transparent upper shield helps keep that airflow close to the face without enclosing the whole body.

Route linked alerts help travellers compare air quality at airports and destinations before booking.

Know the limits

AirHalo does not clean an entire airport or protect everyone nearby. Filtration depends on the device fit, filter condition and outside pollution level.

Route alerts describe environmental risk, but they do not guarantee safe air.

Access notice

Short visit rentals are available at selected airports. During periods of high demand, devices may need to be reserved in advance.

Travellers without a device can still compare lower pollution routes, but these routes may cost more or take longer.
